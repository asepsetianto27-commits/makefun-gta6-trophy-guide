Roadmap to the platinum

Built from the official trophy descriptions. One playthrough is enough, about 200 hours all in (PowerPyx: 4/10 difficulty), and there is no mission replay — so keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter. The 100% checklist stays hidden until after the story, but everything you do during the story already counts toward it.

Missable trophies

Only one trophy is reported as possibly missable. Everything else can be mopped up in free-roam after the story — all trophy-related quest givers are still available then (PowerPyx). Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep a trophy run cheat-free.

How this guide was built

Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 list as published on Exophase and PSNProfiles (55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden until earned; no online trophies). Each guide is written for this entry in our own words from the official description, and names its sources beside it — a source is cited, never copied. Where a description is vague, the guide says so instead of inventing a step. Roadmap order and the missable flag come from the official descriptions plus the early published guide by PowerPyx. No Rockstar logo, artwork or game screenshot is used: the look is our own.

Sources: Exophase — GTA VI trophy list · PSNProfiles — GTA VI trophies · PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap
